## The old rule: Cheap, fast and good. The old rule said pick two.

It held because people’s time was the whole cost: cheap meant rushed, fast meant expensive, good meant slow. AI agents changed the cost of the first pass. They do it in a fraction of the time, for a few dollars. What is left is a person checking the result, and that is the part we never skimp on. So the rule is broken, and you get all three.

## The name: Why Clinamen

![Atoms fall in straight lines; one swerves a little and lands somewhere new.](https://clinamenco.com/img/clinamen-swerve-diagram.webp)

*Fig. 1 · a small lean, a different landing*

**clinamen** /klaɪˈneɪmən/ (kly-nay-men), *noun*, plural clinamina

Latin, from *clinare*, to lean.

1. **The swerve.** In Lucretius’ *De rerum natura*, written around 55 BC, atoms fall straight down through the void until one, at no fixed time or place, leans a little from its path. Without that swerve nothing would ever meet, and nature would never have made a thing.
2. **Us.** A tiny swerve, a huge impact: one chore handed off, and the whole week lands somewhere new. Small swerves for small businesses.

**Say it:** CLI ’n’ Amen. CLI is the command line, where our agents do their work. Amen: so be it.

## Your data: How we handle your data

Data privacy is the first worry most owners raise. Here is what we do.

**What access we ask for.** Only what one job needs. For a missed-call text back, that may be your phone line settings and nothing else. We ask for a test account or sample data first, where we can. We never ask for passwords by email. We use each tool’s own sharing or admin access, so you can remove it any time.

**Where your data is kept.** In your own tools, where possible. Anything we must hold while we work stays in access-controlled accounts that only the people on your job can get into. We never post client data in public or use it in our own marketing.

**Who sees it.** AI agents do the first pass on your job. Evan, the founder, checks every result before you see it.

**When we delete it.** When the work ends, we delete the copies of your data we hold, remove our access, and confirm it in writing.

**Regulated data.** Health records, privileged legal files and financial advice carry their own rules. We scope the safeguards in writing before any work starts.
