The name

clinamen /klaɪˈneɪmən/ noun plural clinamina

Latin, from clinare, to lean.

  1. The swerve. In Lucretius’ De rerum natura, written around 55 BC, atoms fall straight down through the void until one, at no fixed time or place, leans a little from its path. Without that swerve nothing would ever meet, and nature would never have made a thing.
  2. Us. A tiny swerve, a huge impact: one chore handed off, and the whole week lands somewhere new. Small swerves for small businesses.

Say it CLI’n’Amen CLI is the command line, where our agents do their work. Amen: so be it.

How we handle your data

Data privacy is the first worry most owners raise. Here is what we do.

What access we ask for
Only what one job needs. For a missed-call text back, that may be your phone line settings and nothing else. We ask for a test account or sample data first, where we can. We never ask for passwords by email. We use each tool’s own sharing or admin access, so you can remove it any time.
Where your data is kept
In your own tools, where possible. Anything we must hold while we work stays in access-controlled accounts that only the people on your job can get into. We never post client data in public or use it in our own marketing.
Who sees it
AI agents do the first pass on your job. Evan, the founder, checks every result before you see it.
When we delete it
When the work ends, we delete the copies of your data we hold, remove our access, and confirm it in writing.
What we won’t touch
In our first months we don’t take work involving health records (HIPAA), privileged legal files or financial advice.
