## How it works: Draft, approve, and it goes out on time

1. **Connect your accounts.** Instagram, Threads and more, each through the network’s own official connection. It never asks for your main password.
2. **Drafts land in one queue.** Write a post, or drop ready posts into a shared folder for it to pick up. Each draft waits for you, with a preview for every network.
3. **You say yes.** Approve a post to go now or at a set time. Change it after you approve it, and it waits for you again.
4. **It posts, and checks.** Each post goes out at its time and is checked once it’s live. If the time slips by, the post waits for you instead of going out late.

## Why it helps: Your posts, out on time, and never without you

- **Nothing goes out without your yes.** Every post is approved by a person, and every change is logged.
- **One queue for every channel.** Write once, with a version for each network, and see the week in one place.
- **On time, or not at all.** A post goes out in its window, or waits for you. A stale post never goes out on its own.
- **A note every morning.** What went out, what comes next, and what needs you, in one short note.
- **Brands and clients kept apart.** Each brand or client gets its own accounts, posts and settings, with roles for who drafts and who approves.
- **AI that asks first.** On the way: drafts from your own material, for you to approve. It will never post on its own.
