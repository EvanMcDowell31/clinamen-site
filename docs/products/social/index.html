In early development

Social media that waits for your yes

We’re building a tool that plans, drafts and posts to every channel you use, from one place. Its AI will suggest, and you decide: nothing is published, changed or deleted without your approval.

Hear when it’s ready See how it works

For owners who do their own posting, and for the people who post for them.

How it works

Draft, approve, and it goes out on time

  1. 01

    Connect your accounts

    Instagram, Threads and more, each through the network’s own official connection. It never asks for your main password.

  2. 02

    Drafts land in one queue

    Write a post, or drop ready posts into a shared folder for it to pick up. Each draft waits for you, with a preview for every network.

  3. 03

    You say yes

    Approve a post to go now or at a set time. Change it after you approve it, and it waits for you again.

  4. 04

    It posts, and checks

    Each post goes out at its time and is checked once it’s live. If the time slips by, the post waits for you instead of going out late.

Why it helps

Your posts, out on time, and never without you

  • Nothing goes out without your yes

    Every post is approved by a person, and every change is logged.

  • One queue for every channel

    Write once, with a version for each network, and see the week in one place.

  • On time, or not at all

    A post goes out in its window, or waits for you. A stale post never goes out on its own.

  • A note every morning

    What went out, what comes next, and what needs you, in one short note.

  • Brands and clients kept apart

    Each brand or client gets its own accounts, posts and settings, with roles for who drafts and who approves.

  • AI that asks first

    On the way: drafts from your own material, for you to approve. It will never post on its own.

Where it stands

Early, and moving

Works today, in testing

Posting to several networks after approval, scheduling, a shared drafts folder, the morning note, and a workspace for each brand with its own logins.

Next

AI drafts from your own material, a weekly report on what worked, and more networks.
